## Why the recipe has so few prompts

In the strategic offsite, the model assists with a small fraction of the synthesis. The work is interpretation, prioritization, narration, and political reading — all human work.

If you find yourself reaching for more prompts in this recipe, that's a signal. You're trying to outsource judgment that has to stay with you. **Stop, close the model, and write by hand.**
