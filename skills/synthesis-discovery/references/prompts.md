## The two prompts that earn the recipe

If you remember nothing else from the Discovery recipe, remember these two:

### Prompt #2 — Anti-flattening critic

Run this after Prompt #1, every time. Discovery synthesis lives or dies at this step. Skip it and you ship a synthesis that smoothed the spiky quote that was the whole point of the engagement.

### Prompt #3 — Interpretation stress-test

The single most useful prompt in the whole book. Model is a competent steelman and a terrible advocate. Asked to argue against your interpretation, it does it well. Asked to argue for it, it flatters. Always ask it to push back.
