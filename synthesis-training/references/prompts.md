## What the prompts do NOT cover

- The **baseline-to-close pairs** (Step 1) — your hand-written record per participant
- The **personalized paragraph** for each participant (Step 4) — your voice, citing specific moments
- The **flags section** in the cohort analysis (Step 5) — names of below-average participants with framings
- The **named stories** in the one-pager (Step 6) — calibration of detail vs. identifiability

These four are the authorship-heavy moments. The model doesn't draft them.
