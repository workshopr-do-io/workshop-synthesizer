Trigger the `synthesis-training` skill in `full` mode. Honor explicit alternate modes: `cross-functional` (cross-functional alignment workshop), `sales-customer` (sales-team or customer-facing training).

The recipe's 7-step pipeline has four human-authored pauses:
- Step 1 — baseline-to-close pairs per participant (human-only)
- Step 4 — personalized paragraph per participant (the single most authorship-heavy moment in any recipe in this book)
- Step 5 — flags section in the cohort analysis (naming below-average participants)
- Step 6 — named stories in the one-pager (anonymized but specific)

Three audiences, three altitudes, three documents. The temptation to write once and trim never survives the three-times aloud test.

Uses opus per project policy for the multi-audience tiered narration.
